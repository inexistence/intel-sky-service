## Remaining differences and oracle work

All known calibration gaps remain explicitly labeled in `OfficialBehaviorNotes.md` and in the
request-level evidence column of `ProtocolCatalog.md`. The executable `Tools/oracle` case matrix
covers the eleven public Window APIs and low-level `startApp`; the `Oracle backlog` section records
the additional attended ARM cases for action timing, ViewBridge focus behavior, lock/input
intervention, capture backpressure, and exact error mapping. These do not silently expand Intel's
permissions or substitute invented behavior: uncertain cases fail closed or retain the documented
conservative implementation.
